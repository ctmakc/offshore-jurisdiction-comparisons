Offshore jurisdiction comparisons: 10 head-to-head tables

Founders rarely choose a jurisdiction from a list of thirty. They end up weighing two: BVI or Belize for a holding, Delaware or Wyoming for an LLC, Hong Kong or Singapore for Asian trade. This dataset collects ten such pairs and compares them on the parameters a bank, an investor and a tax adviser will ask about: the governing law, the registry, the agent, beneficial ownership filings, annual reporting, tax and the EU list of October 9, 2026. Each table ends with short choice scenarios and links to the full comparison on Offshora.

Compiled from the knowledge base of Offshora. All jurisdiction comparisons on Offshora · Offshore jurisdictions overview

Three typical scenarios

  • A startup with venture plans. Three co-founders from different countries, a round in 12–18 months. A Delaware C-Corp is usually chosen: investors are used to the documents and the court.
  • A consultant with clients in the US and Europe. A company is needed for contracts and invoices, with no outside investors. Wyoming is cheaper to maintain; compare it with a UK company in our article UK Ltd vs US LLC.
  • The team works from a third state. If the business is actually conducted in another state, the company may need to register there as a foreign entity (foreign qualification). A corporation that is foreign to Delaware, for example, files an annual report by June 30 with a $250 fee. This cost is worth budgeting for before choosing a state.
